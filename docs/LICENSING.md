# Licensing

Sooauth is built by Sooapps and is offered in two operating models.

## Community Edition

The server Community Edition is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. It is
intended for teams that want to run authentication on their own infrastructure.
Self-hosting does not require a Sooapps subscription, but operators are
responsible for the deployment, database, email, backups, security updates, and
compliance of their installation.

The JavaScript SDK and third-party dependencies may have separate licenses.
Check package-level license files before redistributing a combined work.

## Hosted Sooauth

The hosted service at `https://auth.sooauth.com` is operated by Sooapps. Users
can sign up, create projects, and integrate without running PostgreSQL, Redis,
SMTP, or the Go server themselves. It is currently available as a free beta.

Managed backups, monitoring, custom domains, support, and SLA options may become
paid service tiers as the hosted product matures.

## Commercial and enterprise terms

Commercial terms may be needed for proprietary distribution, enterprise
support, advanced organization features, SAML/SCIM, or removing product
attribution from hosted surfaces. Contact `hello@sooapps.com` before building a
commercial product around a modified distribution.

This document is a product summary, not legal advice. Review the repository
license, dependency licenses, and your deployment obligations with counsel.
